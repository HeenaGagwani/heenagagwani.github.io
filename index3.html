Cybersecurity Awareness Analysis

Comprehensive analysis encompassing social media attacks, software security, mobile security, and online privacy. The gathered data was subjected to rigorous analysis using SPSS software and Business Intelligence tools. Cronbach's alpha coefficient (α = 0.759), Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin (KMO) test (0.692) , Hypothesis testing, Awareness analysis. Project Outcomes: Software and mobile security significantly impact cybersecurity awareness(p=0.015), and there's a positive link between social media engagement & attacks(p=0.042), No association between people awareness & privacy(p=0.235).
