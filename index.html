March, 2024

Protecting American Environment: Real Time Gunshot Detection

Collected and annotated gunshot audio from Google AudioSet and UrbanSound 8K. Transformed audio data into signals and extracted MFCC coefficients. Trained CNN, RNN, LSTM, and hybrid models on 2-second audio segments. Achieved highest performance with a bidirectional model, attaining 88.94% Test Accuracy and 92.90% Recall. Deployed the best model on Gradio for real-time gunshot detection.

February, 2024

Forecasting Maximum Electricity Demand in Great Britain

Conducted EDA to analyze trends, seasonality, and anomalies in the time series data. Interpolated missing data points to ensure completeness. Performed Classical Decomposition and statistical tests like Pymannkendall and Kruskal-Wallis. Observed ACF and PACF plots, tested stationarity using ADF and KPSS tests. Built ARIMA and SARIMA models, achieving a MAPE of 3.5% with SARIMA(2,0,5)(5,1,2,7).
