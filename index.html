Heena Gagwani

With my journey in Mathematics and learning programming languages, I learnt about Data Science. The power that data holds in transforming businesses today can't be imagined. With technologies available today, businesses can transform themselves by incorporting Data Science. Thus, I, a Data Scientist, helps to transform businesses through Data.

I completed by Bachelors in Mathematics during which I learnt about Data Science. Hence went on to pursue Masters in Data Science from NMIMS, Mumbai. With the support of professors, their knowledge and having right people at my side, I gained knowledge in Key Tools and Techniques through which businesses can transform themselves.

During my Master’s program, I worked as a Summer Intern – Data Scientist at Accenture, where I learned how to derive business value from data. With the guidance of my mentor, I developed a solution that helped reduce losses in one of the organization’s verticals.

From December 2024 to June 2025, I worked as a Research AI Intern at Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited. My contributions included:

  • Developing a Proof of Concept (PoC) for a Recommendation System using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)
  • Integrated Recommendation System with Agentic Chatbot for Ease and Efficieny of Advisors
  • Leading discussions on Text-to-Speech (TTS) (XTTS, ParlourTTS and F5 Models) systems with the team
  • Conducting research on Gesture Generation using reference videos and corresponding audio

This internship significantly strengthened my expertise in the Generative AI domain, particularly in:

  • Graph Neural Networks (GraphSAGE, GCN, GAT)
  • Recommendation Systems
  • Diffusion and Flow Based Models
  • Audio Data Processing and Understanding
  • Video Data Understanding
